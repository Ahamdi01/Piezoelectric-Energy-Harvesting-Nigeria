# Piezoelectric Energy Harvesting: An Untapped Nigerian Resource

## Publication Details
- **Journal:** International Journal of Engineering Research & Technology (IJERT)
- **Publisher:** ESRSA Publications
- **Authors:** Ahamdi Ogiri Miracle et al.
- **DOI:** [10.5281/zenodo.18080793](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.18080793)

---

## Abstract Summary
This paper explores the potential of piezoelectric energy harvesting as a sustainable 
alternative for power generation in Nigeria. The study examines:

- Piezoelectric materials and their energy harvesting mechanisms
- Implementation challenges in the Nigerian context
- Applications in transportation and infrastructure systems
- Viability as a renewable energy solution for a power-deficit nation

---

## Key Contributions
- Comprehensive review of piezoelectric energy harvesting technologies
- Analysis of Nigeria's untapped piezoelectric energy potential
- Evaluation of practical implementation pathways for Nigerian infrastructure
- Case for piezoelectric systems as supplementary renewable energy sources
